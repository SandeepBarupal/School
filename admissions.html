Admissions Guide

Apply for Aliens School’s free learning programs.

Our process is designed for motivated learners who want to build practical skills in technology, creative work and management.

Start Application Check Eligibility

Admission process

A simple, transparent journey from inquiry to placement.

Inquiry

Submit your interest and tell us why you want to learn with Aliens School.

Screening

We review your motivation, skills and fit for one of our learning streams.

Orientation

Successful learners receive program guidance, rules and a weekly rhythm for progress.

Stream placement

Students are placed into Development, Presentation or Management based on their strengths and goals.

Development

Technology builders, developers and security learners.

Presentation

Designers, marketers and content creators.

Management

Project leaders, planners and systems operators.

Eligibility

Admission is open to learners who are serious about practical learning and willing to commit to the process.

Who can apply

Learners with basic reading and writing skills, a genuine desire to learn, and readiness to follow systems and deadlines.

What we value

Curiosity, discipline, responsibility and a willingness to build real work.

Cost and support

The core learning programs are tuition-free and supported by the Aliens Ecosystem.

Aliens School does not charge tuition fees for its education programs. Any additional services or fees will be confirmed independently.
