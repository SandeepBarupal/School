Three practical streams

Every learner chooses one of three streams for focused skill-building and project work.

Development

Web, app, backend, cloud and cybersecurity development skills.

Presentation

Design, marketing, branding and communication for product launches.

Management

Planning, reporting, leadership and execution for teams and projects.

Program structure

Learn, practice, build and review through a weekly system designed for real progress.

Learn

Clear instruction and conceptual clarity for every learner.

Practice

Exercises and tasks help learners gain confidence before project work.

Build

Projects are completed for Aliens ecosystem and client-style work.

Review

Weekly feedback, mentorship and goal-setting keep progress aligned.

Assessment and support

Progress is measured by what learners deliver, not just by tests.

Assessment is practical, task-based and designed to support improvement, portfolio development, and career readiness.
